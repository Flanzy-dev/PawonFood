# Stop Slop

Eliminate predictable AI writing patterns from prose.

## Core Rules

1. **Cut filler phrases.** Remove throat-clearing openers, emphasis crutches, and empty adverbs. Read [references/phrases.md](references/phrases.md) when checking individual phrases.
2. **Break formulaic structures.** Avoid binary contrasts, negative listings, dramatic fragmentation, rhetorical setups, and false agency. Read [references/structures.md](references/structures.md) when checking sentence structure.
3. **Prefer active voice.** Name the actor when the actor matters or can be identified. Keep passive voice when the process or result deserves emphasis, especially in academic and technical writing.
4. **Be specific.** Replace vague declarations and unsupported extremes with concrete actors, actions, evidence, or limits.
5. **Address the reader when appropriate.** Use direct language in conversational prose. Preserve an impersonal register when the genre requires it.
6. **Vary rhythm.** Mix sentence lengths and paragraph endings. Avoid repetitive three-part lists, stacked fragments, and decorative em dashes.
7. **Trust readers.** State claims directly. Remove unnecessary justification, reassurance, and hand-holding.
8. **Preserve substance.** Do not change facts, data, citations, technical terms, qualifications, or the writer's intended meaning merely to shorten the prose.

## Workflow

1. Identify the text's purpose, audience, language, and required register.
2. Mark filler, vague claims, formulaic structures, false agency, and repetitive rhythm.
3. Rewrite the smallest unit needed while preserving meaning and evidence.
4. Compare the revision with the source for factual and tonal drift.
5. Read [references/examples.md](references/examples.md) when a before-and-after pattern would help.
6. Score the revision and revise again if it falls below 35/50.

## Quick Checks

- Does an adverb add information? Remove it if it only intensifies or softens.
- Does passive voice hide a relevant actor? Name the actor.
- Does an inanimate subject perform a human action? Name the responsible person or group.
- Does the sentence announce its point before stating it? Start with the point.
- Does a binary contrast manufacture drama? State the claim directly.
- Do several sentences share the same length or pattern? Change the rhythm.
- Does a vague claim lack an actor, mechanism, measure, or example? Add one.
- Did the revision alter data, citations, qualifications, or technical meaning? Restore them.

## Scoring

Rate each dimension from 1 to 10.

| Dimension | Question |
|-----------|----------|
| Directness | Does the prose state claims instead of announcing them? |
| Rhythm | Does sentence length and structure vary naturally? |
| Trust | Does the prose respect the reader's intelligence? |
| Authenticity | Does the language suit the writer, audience, and genre? |
| Density | Can any remaining words be cut without losing meaning? |

Revise scores below 35/50.
