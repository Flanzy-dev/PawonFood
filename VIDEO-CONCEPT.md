## 1. PROJECT CONTEXT

**Product:** PawonFood

**Product category:** Hyperlocal Community Food Rescue Platform.

**Official slogan:**
"Dari dapur ke dapur. Masak kebanyakan? Bagikan, jangan buang."

**Primary goal:**
Create a 20-second animated advertisement to serve as the opening sequence of a student team's two-minute hackathon pitching video.

The advertisement must immediately communicate the central idea: edible surplus food in one kitchen could become a meaningful meal for someone nearby.

The target audience includes hackathon judges, university students, boarding-house residents, food businesses, and people interested in food sustainability.

The product's initial target area is Pogung, Sleman, near Universitas Gadjah Mada, with potential expansion throughout Yogyakarta.

### The problem

Households, boarding-house residents, and food businesses sometimes have surplus edible food, while nearby people may be looking for affordable meals.

Food that is still edible can become waste when there is no convenient way to connect the people who have it with people who can use it.

### The solution

PawonFood connects local food providers with nearby receivers through a digital platform.

Its intended user journey is:

1. A provider photographs surplus food and creates a listing.
2. Nearby people discover available food through the application's feed or map.
3. A receiver finds and reserves the food.
4. The receiver collects it and confirms pickup through the application's QR workflow.

The product emphasizes local access, community participation, affordability, and responsible food use.

### Product accuracy

Use the attached PRD and actual application assets as the source of truth.

The current application includes a prototype with mock data and simulated interactions. Some capabilities are not yet production-ready.

Therefore:

- Present the animation as an illustrative product advertisement, not evidence of real-world transactions.
- Do not show invented user statistics, testimonials, partnerships, or measured environmental results.
- Do not portray AI image validation as a guarantee of food safety.
- Do not depict simulated QR scanning as an independently verified production transaction.
- Do not invent screens that contradict the actual application UI.
- If actual screenshots are supplied, preserve the visible logo, UI layout, colors, and product identity when recreating them.
